# Islamic Rulings and Fatwas (Comprehensive Classical & Contemporary Reference)

## 1. Fundamentals of Faith & Creed (Aqeedah)
### Question: What constitutes the foundation of Tawheed (Monotheism)?
**Answer:** Tawheed is categorized into three interrelated dimensions:
1. *Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah* (Oneness of Lordship): Affirming Allah alone as Creator, Sustainer, Controller, and Provider of the universe.
2. *Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah* (Oneness of Worship): Directing all acts of worship (prayer, supplication, sacrifice, vows, hope, fear, and reliance) exclusively to Allah without partners or intermediaries.
3. *Tawheed al-Asma was-Sifat* (Oneness of Names and Attributes): Believing in all beautiful names and divine attributes revealed in the Quran and authentic Sunnah without alteration (*tahrif*), denial (*ta'til*), questioning how (*takyif*), or comparison to creation (*tamthil*).

## 2. Prayer & Purification (Salah & Taharah)
### Question: Can women lead prayer?
**Answer:** In Islamic jurisprudence:
- Women may lead other women in congregational prayer, standing in the middle of the first row rather than forward.
- In mixed congregations of men and women, the consensus of the four Sunni legal schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) is that prayer must be led by a qualified adult Muslim male.

### Question: What is the ruling on wiping over leather socks or shoes (Masah alal-Khuffayn)?
**Answer:** Wiping over leather socks or thick, waterproof footwear is an established Sunnah with unanimous consensus (*Ijma*):
- Must be put on after completing full ablution (wudu) with washed feet.
- Valid for 24 hours for a resident (*Muqim*) and 72 hours for a traveler (*Musafir*).
- Wiping is performed with moist fingers across the top of the foot once.

### Question: What are the conditions for combining and shortening prayers (Jam' and Qasr)?
**Answer:** A traveler journeying a minimum distance of approximately 48 miles (77 km) away from home may shorten four-rak'ah prayers (Dhuhr, Asr, Isha) to two rak'ahs. Combining Dhuhr with Asr, and Maghrib with Isha, is permissible during active travel, heavy torrential rain, severe storms, or necessity according to the majority of scholars. Fajr can never be combined or shortened.

## 3. Financial Transactions & Modern Economics (Mu'amalat)
### Question: What is the Islamic ruling on interest, banking interest, and usury (Riba)?
**Answer:** Riba (usury/interest) is strictly prohibited by explicit Quranic texts (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:275-279) and unanimous scholarly consensus. This prohibition encompasses both *Riba al-Fadl* (unequal exchange of commodities) and *Riba an-Nasi'ah* (interest on loans or deferred obligations). Conventional bank interest on loans, mortgages, and fixed-deposit earnings is considered Riba. Muslims are advised to utilize equity-based Islamic finance (Murabahah, Musharakah, Mudarabah, and Ijarah).

### Question: Is trading cryptocurrencies and digital stocks permissible in Islam?
**Answer:**
- **Stocks & Equities:** Permissible provided the company's primary business activity is halal (avoiding conventional banking, alcohol, pork, gambling, adult media) and financial ratios meet Shariah compliance standards (debt-to-market-cap under 33%, interest income under 5%).
- **Cryptocurrencies:** Scholars allow digital assets that serve a recognized utility, real-world blockchain technology, and store of value, provided they are not associated with gambling, speculative pump-and-dump fraud, or illicit money laundering. Highly speculative margin trading, short-selling, and CFD contracts containing excessive uncertainty (*Gharar*) remain impermissible.

## 4. Zakat and Charity (Zakat & Sadaqah)
### Question: How is Zakat calculated on modern assets (Cash, Gold, Stocks, Crypto, Real Estate)?
**Answer:**
1. **Gold & Silver:** 2.5% of market value if owning 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver.
2. **Liquid Cash & Bank Accounts:** 2.5% on all balances held above Nisab for one full lunar year.
3. **Tradable Stocks & Crypto:** 2.5% of total portfolio market value if held for short-term trading. If held as long-term investment for dividends, zakat applies only to the underlying liquid/inventory assets or net dividend income.
4. **Real Estate:** Personal residence and long-term capital appreciation land are exempt. Rental properties incur Zakat only on the net accumulated rental income held after one year, not the building value itself.

## 5. Fasting & Ramadan (Sawm)
### Question: Who is exempt from fasting in Ramadan, and what are their obligations?
**Answer:**
1. **Travelers & Temporarily Sick:** Exempt from fasting during the condition; they must make up the missed days (*Qada*) day-for-day before the next Ramadan.
2. **Chronically Ill & Elderly:** Those medically unable to fast with no hope of recovery are exempt; they pay *Fidyah* (feeding one poor person two daily meals per missed day).
3. **Pregnant & Nursing Mothers:** If they fear harm to their own health or their child, they may break their fast and make up the days later according to the majority of jurists.
4. **Menstruating & Post-Natal Women:** Prohibited from fasting; they must make up the exact number of missed days after Ramadan without paying fidyah.

### Question: Do injections, blood tests, eye drops, or inhalers invalidate the fast?
**Answer:**
- **Injections:** Non-nutritive injections (intramuscular or intravenous vaccines, antibiotics, insulin, pain relievers) do not break the fast. Nutritive intravenous drips (glucose, vitamins intended to substitute meals) invalidate the fast.
- **Blood Tests & Cupping:** Standard diagnostic blood draws do not break the fast.
- **Asthma Inhalers:** Contemporary Islamic Fiqh Academies hold that standard inhalers do not break the fast because the microscopic medication goes to the respiratory tract rather than the stomach.
- **Eye & Ear Drops:** Do not invalidate the fast even if a taste reaches the throat, as the eye and ear are not standard digestive pathways.

## 6. Contemporary Medical & Bioethical Rulings
### Question: What is the Islamic stance on organ donation and organ transplantation?
**Answer:** The International Islamic Fiqh Academy and major fatwa councils have ruled that organ donation is permissible and meritorious as an act of continuous charity (*Sadaqah Jariyah*), provided:
1. The donor gives informed, voluntary consent without financial remuneration or commercial trafficking.
2. For living donors, the donation will not cause severe harm or death to the donor (e.g. donating one kidney).
3. For deceased donors, brain death or cardiac death is certified by medical experts in accordance with strict legal standards.

### Question: What is the ruling on In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) and assisted reproduction?
**Answer:** IVF is permissible in Islam under strict conditions:
- The sperm and ovum must come exclusively from the legally married husband and wife.
- Implantation must take place within the biological wife's womb during the valid subsistence of marriage.
- Surrogacy, third-party sperm donation, and egg donation are strictly prohibited to preserve legitimate lineage (*Nasab*).

## 7. Dietary & Lifestyle Rulings
### Question: Is music and playing musical instruments permissible in Islam?
**Answer:**
- The vast majority of classical jurists across the four legal schools prohibit instrumental music based on hadiths in Sahih al-Bukhari regarding stringed instruments and windpipes.
- Singing with noble, devotional, educational themes and percussion instruments like the *Duff* during Eid celebrations and wedding feasts is unanimously permissible.
- A minority view (including Imam Ibn Hazm and contemporary scholars like Sheikh Yusuf al-Qaradawi) permits clean music that does not incite lust, vice, alcohol consumption, or distract from obligatory prayers.

### Question: What is the ruling on smoking, vaping, and hookah?
**Answer:** Modern Islamic scholarship across Al-Azhar, the Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research (Saudi Arabia), and European Fatwa Councils unanimously hold that cigarette smoking, electronic vaping, and shisha are **Haram** (prohibited) based on the Quranic principle: *"And make not your own hands contribute to your destruction"* (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:195) and the hadith: *"There should be neither harming nor reciprocating harm"* (Ibn Majah).
