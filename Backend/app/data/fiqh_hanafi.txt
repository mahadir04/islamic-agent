Five Pillars of Islam
1. Shahadah (Declaration of Faith): Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh (There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is His servant and Messenger).
2. Salah (Prayer): Five mandatory daily prayers established at prescribed times facing the Qiblah in Mecca.
3. Zakat (Purification Charity): 2.5% of net surplus wealth meeting or exceeding the Nisab threshold held for an entire lunar year (Hawl).
4. Sawm (Fasting): Complete abstention from food, drink, and intimacy from the break of dawn (Fajr) until sunset (Maghrib) during the holy month of Ramadan.
5. Hajj (Pilgrimage): The pilgrimage to the Sacred House in Mecca performed once in a lifetime for every Muslim who has financial and physical capability.

Prayer Times & Conditions
- Fajr: Starts from true dawn (al-fajr al-sadiq) until just before the rise of the sun disk.
- Dhuhr: Begins right after the sun passes the meridian (zawal) until an object's shadow equals its length plus shadow at noon (or twice according to Imam Abu Hanifah's primary view).
- Asr: Starts when Dhuhr time ends until just before the setting of the sun.
- Maghrib: Starts immediately following sunset when the disk of the sun completely vanishes until the red twilight (shafaq) disappears.
- Isha: Begins when twilight completely disappears from the sky until true dawn (Fajr); preferably offered in the first third of the night.
- Witr: Strongly emphasized (Wajib in the Hanafi school), offered after Isha before dawn, consisting of three rak'ahs with Dua al-Qunoot.

Purification & Taharah
Wudu (Ablution) Obligatory Fard Acts (4 according to the Quran 5:6):
1. Washing the entire face once from hairline to chin and from earlobe to earlobe.
2. Washing both arms including elbows once.
3. Wiping at least one-fourth of the head once with wet hands.
4. Washing both feet including the ankles once.

Wudu Sunnah & Recommended Steps:
1. Make the intention (Niyyah) in the heart.
2. Say Bismillah before beginning.
3. Wash both hands up to the wrists three times, cleaning between fingers.
4. Rinse the mouth thoroughly three times (Madmadah).
5. Inhale water into the nostrils and blow out three times (Istinshaq).
6. Wash the face three times.
7. Wash arms up to and including elbows three times, starting with the right.
8. Wipe the entire head with wet hands from front to back, including wiping the ears and back of the neck.
9. Wash both feet up to and including the ankles three times, starting with the right, passing wet fingers between toes.

Nullifiers of Wudu (Nawaqid):
- Discharge of urine, feces, wind, or any substance from the private paths.
- Flowing of blood, pus, or serum from any wound past the point of exit.
- Vomiting a mouthful of bile, food, or water.
- Sleeping lying down or leaning heavily against support such that one would fall if removed.
- Loss of consciousness, fainting, intoxication, or severe insanity.
- Laughing aloud (Qahqahah) during a prayer that includes Ruku and Sujud.

Ghusl (Ritual Bath) Mandatory Requirements:
1. Thoroughly rinsing the entire mouth with water.
2. Inhaling water into the nose to clean thoroughly.
3. Washing the entire body with water such that not a single hair or skin patch remains dry.

Salah (Prayer) Structure & Rak'ahs
- Fajr: 2 Sunnah Mu'akkadah + 2 Fard.
- Dhuhr: 4 Sunnah Mu'akkadah + 4 Fard + 2 Sunnah Mu'akkadah (+ 2 Nafl recommended).
- Asr: 4 Sunnah Ghayr-Mu'akkadah (recommended) + 4 Fard.
- Maghrib: 3 Fard + 2 Sunnah Mu'akkadah (+ 2 Nafl recommended).
- Isha: 4 Sunnah Ghayr-Mu'akkadah + 4 Fard + 2 Sunnah Mu'akkadah + 3 Witr Wajib (+ 2 Nafl).
- Jumu'ah (Friday): Replaces Dhuhr for adult males in congregation, preceded by two Khutbahs followed by 2 Fard rak'ahs.

Essential Pillars (Arkan) of Salah:
1. Takbirat al-Ihram (Saying Allahu Akbar to start).
2. Qiyam (Standing posture for those physically able).
3. Qira'ah (Recitation of Quran, minimally one long ayah or three short ayahs; reciting Surah Al-Fatiha is Wajib).
4. Ruku (Bowing with hands on knees and back straight, reciting Subhana Rabbiyal Azeem).
5. Sujud (Two prostrations on seven bones: forehead/nose, both hands, both knees, and toes of both feet, reciting Subhana Rabbiyal A'la).
6. Qa'dah Akhirah (Final sitting long enough to recite Tashahhud).

Zakat Calculations & Nisab
Conditions for Zakat Obligation:
- Must be a Muslim, free, sane, and adult (according to Hanafi, children's wealth is not zakatable until puberty).
- Full legal ownership and possession of productive or potentially productive wealth.
- Wealth must exceed basic necessities of life (housing, transportation, work tools, clothing, debts).
- Wealth must meet or exceed the Nisab benchmark.
- Wealth must be possessed for one complete lunar year (Hawl).

Nisab Thresholds:
- Gold Nisab: 20 Mithqals equivalent to 85 grams of pure gold.
- Silver Nisab: 200 Dirhams equivalent to 595 grams of pure silver.
- Cash & Trade Assets: Value equivalent to the silver nisab (or gold according to conservative contemporary scholars).
- Rate of Zakat: 2.5% (one-fortieth) of total qualifying liquid savings and trade inventory.
- Agricultural Produce (Ushr): 10% on naturally watered crops (rain/rivers); 5% on crops watered through irrigation expenses.
- Livestock: Specific fixed schedules for sheep, goats, cattle, and camels.

Eligible Recipients of Zakat (Asnaf - Surah At-Tawbah 9:60):
1. Al-Fuqara (The poor with little to no income).
2. Al-Masakin (The destitute who do not have enough for daily survival).
3. Al-Amilina Alayha (Appointed Zakat administrators and collectors).
4. Al-Mu'allafatu Qulubuhum (Those whose hearts are inclined toward Islam).
5. Fir-Riqab (Freeing captives and slaves).
6. Al-Gharimin (Debtors unable to pay legitimate debts).
7. Fi Sabilillah (In the cause of Allah for education, defense, and righteous causes).
8. Ibn as-Sabil (The stranded traveler cut off from resources).

Sawm (Fasting) In Ramadan
Obligatory Conditions:
- Intention (Niyyah) made before dawn (or before Islamic midday for voluntary fasts).
- Complete abstinence from eating, drinking, medication, smoking, and sexual intercourse.

Valid Exemptions from Fasting:
- Traveling a distance of roughly 48 miles (77 km) or more.
- Acute or chronic illness where fasting would worsen health or delay recovery.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding where maternal or infant health would be harmed.
- Advanced old age with frailty (repayable via Fidyah).
- Menstruation (Hayd) and post-natal bleeding (Nifas) (fasting is prohibited; days must be made up after Ramadan without penalty).

Expiation for Broken Fasts:
- Qada: Making up missed or broken fasts day-for-day.
- Kaffarah: Required when a fast is deliberately broken without valid excuse through food, drink, or intercourse: Freeing a slave, or fasting 60 consecutive days, or feeding 60 poor people.
- Fidyah: Feeding one poor person two meals (or monetary equivalent of Sadaqat al-Fitr) for each day missed by one who has a permanent inability to fast.

Hajj & Umrah Essential Rites
Obligatory Fard Acts of Hajj:
1. Ihram (Entering sacred state with intention and Talbiyah).
2. Wuquf at Arafat (Standing on the plains of Arafat on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah between Dhuhr and sunset).
3. Tawaf al-Ziyarah / Ifadah (Circumambulating the Kaaba 7 times after the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah).

Wajib Acts of Hajj:
1. Staying at Muzdalifah during the night of 10th Dhul-Hijjah.
2. Sa'i between Safa and Marwah (7 laps).
3. Rami al-Jamarat (Stoning the pillars at Mina).
4. Halq (Shaving the head) or Taqsir (Clipping the hair).
5. Tawaf al-Wada (Farewell circumambulation for non-residents of Mecca).
6. Qurbani (Animal sacrifice) for those performing Tamattu or Qiran.

Nikah (Marriage) & Family Law
Conditions for Valid Islamic Marriage (Nikah):
1. Mutual consent and acceptance (Ijab and Qabul) expressed in clear wording.
2. Presence of at least two sane, adult Muslim male witnesses (or one male and two females in Hanafi fiqh).
3. Consent and representation of the guardian (Wali).
4. Specified gift from groom to bride (Mahr/Dowry) as her exclusive property.
5. Freedom from legal impediments (e.g. foster-milk kinship, existing four wives, blood prohibitions).

Halal & Haram Dietary Guidelines
Permissible (Halal):
- Herbivorous animals slaughtered according to Islamic rites (Dhabihah): Invoking the name of Allah (Bismillah Allahu Akbar) and cutting the jugular veins, carotid arteries, and windpipe without severing the spinal cord.
- All seafood and marine life (fish, scales, and clean sea creatures).
- Fruits, vegetables, grains, legumes, and honey.

Prohibited (Haram):
- Carrion (dead animals not ritually slaughtered).
- Flowing blood.
- Swine (pork and all derivatives, gelatin, enzymes).
- Animals dedicated to other than Allah.
- Carnivorous animals with fangs (lions, wolves, dogs, cats) and birds of prey with talons (eagles, hawks).
- Domesticated donkeys.
- Intoxicants of all forms (alcohol, narcotics, mind-altering recreational drugs) in small or large quantities.
